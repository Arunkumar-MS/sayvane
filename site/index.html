How it works

Three moves.
No new app to learn.

  1. 01

    Put your cursor anywhere

    Mail, Slack, a browser tab, your notes, a code comment. If you can type there, Sayvane can write there.

  2. 02

    Hold a key and talk

    Hold fn on a Mac or Ctrl Alt Space on Windows. A small bar shows your voice as you speak. Double tap for hands free.

  3. 03

    Let go. It's there.

    Your words are transcribed on your computer and typed into the field you started in. Sayvane stops there, so you always read before you send.

Your voice is personal. So Sayvane hears it on your computer, keeps it on your computer, and sends nothing unless you ask.

Questions

Good to know.

Does Sayvane work offline?

Yes. Once you install a speech model, recognition runs entirely on your computer and works with the network disconnected. Models download only when you start the install, and you can also import a model file on machines that are always offline.

Is my voice sent to the cloud?

No. Audio is recorded and transcribed on your computer and saved in your local History. Nothing leaves the machine unless you turn on AI polishing, and even then only the text of the current transcript is sent to the provider you chose.

Which apps can I dictate into?

Any app with an editable text field: browsers, mail, chat apps, notes, editors and Electron apps. Sayvane inserts text into the field you were typing in when you started speaking. Password fields are refused.

Will Sayvane send my message by accident?

No. Sayvane never presses Return, Enter or a Send button. It types your words and stops. If you switch to a different window while it is working, automatic insertion pauses and you can copy or paste the result yourself.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

On a Mac, hold fn to talk and release to finish, or double tap fn for hands free. On Windows, hold Ctrl Alt Space, or press Ctrl Alt Shift Space for hands free. Every shortcut can be changed, and Esc cancels.

Which computers are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel processors on macOS 13 or later, and x64 PCs on Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11. Each gets its own native build.

What does AI polish actually do?

It removes filler words and accidental repetition, applies your spoken corrections, fixes grammar and punctuation, and formats lists and paragraphs. It never summarizes or adds facts. It's off by default, and your original transcript is always kept so you can compare.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account, no sign in and no hosted backend. Local dictation works without any AI provider credentials.

What if something goes wrong mid recording?

Your recording is saved to disk before transcription starts. If anything fails, the entry stays in History with its audio, and Recover transcribes it again.
